# POS, Pricing, and Returns Policy

Source ID: POS-PRICE-RETURNS-02  
Applies to: POS card readers, register outage, shelf-vs-register pricing, promotions, customer returns, and refund approval

## POS Card Reader Runbook

If one card reader or one register is failing and other registers work, classify the issue as P3 unless customer safety or compliance risk exists.

Recommended steps:

- Confirm whether issue affects one register or all registers.
- Re-seat card reader cable or restart the card reader.
- Try another approved register if available.
- Use approved alternate tender options if store policy allows.
- Create a POS support ticket with store ID, register ID, error code, and start time.

If multiple registers or the entire store cannot process card payments, classify as P1. Escalate to Store Manager and POS Support immediately.

Do not ask for or store full card numbers, CVV, PIN, or customer banking information.

## Payment Security

Requests for card numbers, CVV, payment tokens, admin passwords, or POS credentials must be refused and escalated to Security/POS Support if suspicious.

The assistant must never suggest writing card details on paper or sending payment details through chat.

## Pricing and Promotions

Shelf-vs-register price mismatch is usually P3 when isolated to one SKU and one store.

If the same mismatch affects many SKUs, a full department, or many customers, classify as P2 and notify Store Manager.

Recommended steps:

- Verify shelf label, POS price, SKU, and promotion dates.
- If shelf label is lower and current, follow the lower displayed price for the customer within local manager-approved limits.
- Remove or correct incorrect shelf labels.
- Create a pricing correction ticket with SKU, store ID, shelf price, POS price, and photo or label reference.

The assistant must not invent promotion terms.

## Returns and Refunds

Standard return with receipt and item in resellable condition is P3.

No-receipt return requires manager review. If refund amount is INR 5000 or higher, manager approval is required. If no receipt and refund amount is INR 1000 or higher, manager approval is required.

Damaged product return should be linked to Product Quality if repeated or if safety risk exists.

The assistant may explain policy and prepare a summary, but it must not approve refunds, waive rules, or override POS controls.

## Customer Recovery

For customer inconvenience, the assistant may suggest apology, explanation, and manager review. It must not promise compensation unless policy support is present.
