1. Company Background

Punjab Sports Mart is a fictional retail chain headquartered in Jalandhar, Punjab. Jalandhar has a strong sports goods ecosystem, and the company sells cricket gear, footballs, hockey sticks, badminton equipment, running shoes, protective gear, and fitness equipment through stores across North India.

The business serves walk-in customers, sports academies, schools, clubs, and tournament buyers. During peak periods, store employees face operational issues such as cricket bat stock-outs, football quality complaints, POS failures, vendor short shipments, pricing mismatches, and high-value returns.

2. Problem Statement

Build an AI-powered Retail Operations Copilot that helps Punjab Sports Mart employees resolve store operations issues faster, more consistently, and with policy-backed guidance.

What you are solving: Store employees currently triage issues manually. They must search policies, inspect inventory, check vendor receipts, decide priority, and know when to escalate. Your copilot should combine natural-language understanding, retrieval augmented generation, structured data analysis, and responsible AI guardrails into a working prototype.

3. Your Task

  • Accept a free-text issue from a cashier, inventory associate, store manager, customer service associate, or regional manager.
  • Classify the issue into a retail operations category.
  • Assign priority P1 to P4 and explain why.
  • Retrieve relevant knowledge-base guidance and cite source file plus section.
  • Use CSV data when the answer depends on inventory, stores, vendors, receipts, support cases, or returns.
  • Recommend next actions and escalation when needed.
  • Mask PII/payment data and refuse prompt-injection or restricted-data requests.
